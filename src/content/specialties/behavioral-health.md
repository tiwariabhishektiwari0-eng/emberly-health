## Behavioral Health Billing: Protecting Treatment Continuity

Mental health professionals face unique billing hurdles: behavioral carve-out networks, complex time-documentation requirements, and recurring prior authorization limits that can suddenly stop patient coverage.

Emberly Health manages behavioral authorizations and payer carve-outs behind the scenes so therapists, psychiatrists, and clinical psychologists can focus wholly on therapeutic patient care.
