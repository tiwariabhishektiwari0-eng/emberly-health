## Stopping Denials Before the Patient Reaches the Exam Room

Over 20% of all medical claim denials are rooted in **front-end intake errors**: an inactive insurance policy, an unverified secondary payer, or a procedure performed without required prior authorization. These denials are 100% preventable.

Emberly Health acts as your practice’s digital gatekeeper. By verifying coverage details and securing authorizations 48 hours in advance, we protect both your practice’s cash flow and your patient’s financial peace of mind.
