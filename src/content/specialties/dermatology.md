## Dermatology Billing: Mastering Lesion Excision Margins

In dermatology, code selection hinges on precise millimeters: the lesion diameter plus the narrowest clinical margins, anatomical location, and pathology findings. Billing before the surgical pathology report returns often causes downcoding or claim rejection.

Emberly Health synchronizes pathology reconciliation with charge release, maximizing your revenue on complex closures, flap repairs, and Mohs stages.
