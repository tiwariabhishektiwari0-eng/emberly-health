## Physical Therapy Billing: Protecting Every Timed Minute

In outpatient physical and occupational therapy, reimbursement is strictly governed by minutes on the clock. Violating the CMS 8-minute rule, exceeding therapy threshold caps without a justified KX modifier, or letting a referring physician's Plan of Care signature lapse will immediately freeze clinic revenue.

Emberly Health acts as your clinic’s compliance partner, auditing timed units against total encounter documentation before claims ever leave our clearinghouse.
