## Radiology Billing: Precision Across Modalities

Radiology practices handle massive claim volumes with tight turnaround demands. Misaligning a referring physician's NPI, omitting an Appropriate Use Criteria (AUC) decision support indicator, or misapplying a professional/technical component modifier can immediately derail thousands of claims.

Emberly Health’s radiology billing pod ensures clean batch processing with automated order reconciliation and robust interventional coding support.
