## Eliminating Clearinghouse Lag for US Practices

In modern healthcare revenue management, the difference between stable cash flow and operational stress lies in **first-pass claim acceptance**. When a billing submission stalls at the clearinghouse level due to a minor demographic discrepancy or misplaced taxonomy code, payment is delayed by an average of 21 to 45 days.

Emberly Health takes direct operational ownership of your electronic billing queue. Our US billing specialists establish rigorous claim scrubbing rules calibrated to the nuanced requirements of regional commercial payers, Medicare MACs, and Medicaid managed care organizations.

### Why Front-End Scrubbing Matters
A claim rejected at the clearinghouse level never enters payer adjudication. By addressing front-end rejections within hours rather than weeks, our billing team keeps your cash cycle predictable and reduces aged account buildup.
