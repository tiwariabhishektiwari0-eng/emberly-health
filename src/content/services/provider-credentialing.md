## Eliminating the Out-of-Network Credentialing Trap

Nothing disrupts practice cash flow faster than a newly hired physician treating patients before their commercial payer contracts are fully executed. Claims submitted for non-credentialed providers result in immediate out-of-network denials, patient confusion, or costly uncollectible write-offs.

Emberly Health manages the entire credentialing lifecycle with military precision. We navigate the bureaucratic labyrinth of CAQH, PECOS, and commercial health plan committees so your providers can generate revenue from day one.
