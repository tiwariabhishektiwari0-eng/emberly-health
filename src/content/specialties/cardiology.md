## Cardiology Billing: Navigating Complex Vascular Bundles

Cardiology billing is among the most clinically sophisticated sectors in US healthcare finance. Navigating the anatomical hierarchies of coronary and peripheral interventions requires coders who understand hemodynamic catheterization reports as well as CMS National Coverage Determinations.

Emberly Health’s cardiology pod prevents devastating downcoding and recoupments through daily audit checks on coronary interventions, device implants, and non-invasive diagnostic studies.
