## Harmonizing Cash Reconciliation and Patient Satisfaction

In today’s high-deductible healthcare landscape, patient financial responsibility accounts for an estimated 25% to 35% of a medical practice's total gross revenue. Yet managing patient collections in-house is often uncomfortable for clinical staff and confusing for patients.

Emberly Health bridges this gap with rigorous cash ledger posting and compassionate, clear patient communication. We ensure every penny paid by insurers and patients is accounted for on your balance sheet while safeguarding the physician-patient relationship.
