## Orthopedic Billing: Defending High-Acuity Surgical Margins

Orthopedic surgeons face high procedure values paired with stringent commercial payer scrutiny. A single unbilled implant component, an improper post-operative modifier, or an unverified hospital surgical pre-authorization can result in tens of thousands of dollars in lost practice revenue.

Emberly Health’s orthopedic billing specialists read complex operative notes with surgical precision, ensuring full reimbursement across ambulatory surgery centers (ASC), hospital inpatient stays, and in-office injection encounters.
