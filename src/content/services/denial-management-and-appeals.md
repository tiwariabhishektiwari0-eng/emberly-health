## Turning Lost Claims Into Recovered Practice Revenue

Commercial and government payers deny an estimated 10% to 15% of all healthcare claims submitted in the United States. Worse still, industry studies demonstrate that over **60% of denied claims are never appealed** by busy in-house practice staff due to lack of time and complex appeal rules.

At Emberly Health, we treat every denied claim as an unresolved debt owed to your practice. Our denial management team combines technological categorization with clinical appeal expertise to overturn denials swiftly and systematically.
