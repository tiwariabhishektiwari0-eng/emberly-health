## Primary Care Billing: Capturing Value in High-Volume Practice

Primary care practices operate at high patient volume where small coding omissions compound into massive annual revenue losses. From unbilled bedside rapid tests and immunizations to missed Chronic Care Management (CCM) minutes, revenue leakage in primary care is predominantly front-end and documentation-based.

Emberly Health pairs family medicine and internal medicine practices with certified coders who understand the documentation nuances required to substantiate concurrent preventative and acute care encounters.
