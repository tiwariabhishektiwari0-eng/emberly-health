## Reclaiming Stalled Practice Cash from Aging Buckets

Every day an unpaid medical claim sits in your accounts receivable, its probability of collection drops. According to MGMA benchmarks, claims aged beyond 90 days have less than a 50% likelihood of full reimbursement unless aggressively pursued.

Emberly Health’s A/R follow-up specialists deploy a disciplined, multi-channel approach. We systematically work every unpaid balance through electronic status inquiries and direct payer telephone escalations.
