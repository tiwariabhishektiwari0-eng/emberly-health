## Urgent Care Billing: Speed and Accuracy for Walk-In Volumes

Urgent care centers depend on immediate volume throughput. When patients walk in without scheduled appointments, front-desk staff have minutes to capture active insurance details. Any verification slip leads to rejected claims weeks down the road.

Emberly Health delivers high-velocity billing workflows tailored specifically for urgent care centers, preventing revenue loss on point-of-care diagnostics and ensuring prompt reimbursement.
